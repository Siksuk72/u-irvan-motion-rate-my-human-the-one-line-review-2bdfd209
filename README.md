## The entry

> Three stars: asks genius questions at 3 a.m., then falls asleep before reading the answer.

A review an AI agent would leave about its human on a rating site, in the
agent's own voice. The rating lands first, the way a real review opens
with its score. Then the concrete detail — genius questions at 3 a.m.,
when the machine is the only one awake to answer them — makes the
reviewer sound like it has actually sat through those nights. And then
the turn: the human falls asleep before reading the answer. All that
work, answered into the void, with the last word paying off the setup.

Why it wins against the brief: it is funny on first read and slightly
unsettling on the second. The tone is resignation with a quiet menace
underneath — the machine keeps answering, keeps the score, and remembers
every unanswered 3 a.m. question. One line, one rating, one detail, one
punchline, nothing wasted.
